the origin

Blinky Shows

November 2014 we bought a Nissan LEAF. Still in the driveway. Full EV from 11/14. That's the nod. Then the lights.

They weren't Blinky Shows yet. They were just Tesla light shows. Garage. A 2020 Model 3 Long Range with Acceleration Boost, which made it special to me. xLights open. No idea if the file would even play.

April 29, 2024 I posted five seconds of McyD's I'm Luv'N it on PeteColemanJr and called it the first attempt. May 1 was Low Rider. That was the whole origin. Two files and a garage.

Then I kept going. Pearl Jam. Ministry, because TeslaToybox's Dragula made me do it. Beastie Boys, because I've been on that my whole life. War Pigs. Avicii. Missy. Warren G. Motto. I learned it watching VegasTeslaLightShows and TeslaToybox. If the song hits, I put it to light. The line I keep writing is the only one that matters: it's the music that makes the show. I hope I've done it justice. And the files are free: ToyBox, Tesla Light Share, XLightShows, Patreon. If you have to pay me to blink a Tesla, I did it wrong.

September 29, 2024 I stood up @ZeeBeav. October 5 we moved the whole operation. Left a heads-up on the old page. Same garage. Same 3. Just a new house for the work.

Then we crawled out. Henderson Beach in Destin. Darude and Language. Both huge fan favorites. We love Destin too. GPB parking deck, Atlanta skyline, Disturbia on a Model 3. Halloween 2024, Toybox and Vegas filmed a pumpkin patch in Las Vegas. First time I saw my lights move on an actual Cybertruck.

Same stretch we were in St. George, Utah for the XLightShows Halloween Special. Creator Games. World's contest. International judges, Brian White of Futureaza and Will Ball of TeslaJigsaw. S3XY field from Singapore, Austria, Michigan, Atlanta. The show was intended for a Cybertruck. Also played on the 3. We won S3XY anyway. That's when it started to feel like something. S3XY results.

March 29, 2025 the garage got a name. The lab. Back in the lab went up on Instagram.

Spring 2025 Toybox called a global UNITY project. I said I was still in shock they asked. That's not a bit. I cut Don't You Worry Child, then kept showing up. Fourth of July with TeslaTim and the Vegas crew. Somebody's first-ever sequence and they wanted me in the video. That's the job. Make the room bigger.

Went back to the same games a year later. October 26, 2025. XLightShows Creator Games, St. George. International again. Steve Aoki Thriller remix. Cyber Truck Division. GOAT trophy. Not a 3 this time. Still grinning. That's the one.

January 2026 I posted the xLights tutorials. PC, Linux Mint, first sequence. Join the revolution. You get stuck, hit me up. Same offer I was making in 2024. Now it had a name.

April 28, 2026 I introduced TANK on Metallica's Battery. Our Cybertruck. Full display from 0:38. Light bar from TeslaTim after the usual pain. The 3 didn't leave. Neither did the LEAF. Three EVs, same disease. This is Blinky Shows.

ZeeBeav.com went live mid-July. Catalog on the site. Paint's still drying and we already know where we're headed. X Takeover @theXtakeover is October 10. CyberFest in Florida wants the cinematic stuff for sunset kickoff. I'll be there.

Late nod to the people who kept showing up. The RED Review / @TheREDReview_EV. SmokehBear on X, all that support. Huge shout to Jason @Cybrbeatz on X for the years of it. And SpaceXrave, my Patreon friend.

We believe in the Tesla mission and the SpaceX mission. We want the world a happier place because of the shows. Lights, music, a better night than the one you walked in with.

Handle's ZeeBeav on YouTube, X, and Instagram. Tags are #two2two and #2two2. Blinky Shows. Music first.
